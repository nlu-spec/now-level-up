A brand mission states what you do for others and the approach you follow as you aim to achieve the aspirations you've set for yourself, or business. Your mission is a route to follow to achieve your vision.

What is Brand Purpose?

A mission statement defines what an organization is, why it exists, its reason for being. The mission statement articulates the company's purpose both for those in the organization and for the public.
