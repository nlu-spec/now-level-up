FAQs Of PPC Services

Is PPC harder than SEO?

PPC isn’t harder, but it’s faster-paced. SEO builds long-term visibility, while PPC needs constant monitoring, bidding, and optimisation for immediate results.

How to improve PPC campaigns?

Refine keywords, use targeted ad copy, optimise landing pages, test different creatives, and regularly review performance through PPC campaign analysis.

What are the types of PPC campaigns?

Common types include search ads, display ads, shopping ads, video ads, and remarketing, each designed for different stages of user intent.

How do PPC campaigns work?

PPC works on a pay-per-click model where advertisers bid on keywords, and ads appear when users search relevant terms.

What are the 4 assumptions of PPC?

They include clear goals, defined audience targeting, relevant keywords, and ongoing data analysis to ensure an effective PPC campaign.
