What is the Difference Between an Illustrator and a Graphic Designer?

An illustrator focuses on creating original artwork, bringing ideas to life through drawings, paintings, or digital illustrations. A graphic designer, on the other hand, arranges text, images, and design elements to communicate messages visually, often using existing graphics. While both fields overlap, illustration is more artistic, and graphic design is more structured and layout-driven.
