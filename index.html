What are your areas of focus as a leading UI/UX design company in India?

We focus on end-to-end UI/UX design — from research and wireframes to high-fidelity interfaces and design systems — across web and mobile. With 170+ projects delivered, our work spans Fintech, Healthtech, Foodtech, Traveltech, Edtech, E-commerce, Real estate tech and AI-driven products.

What separates Now Level Up from other top UI/UX design agencies?

We combine design craft with behavior science and neuromarketing insight, so every screen is built to look good and perform. Add to that transparent communication, a dedicated in-house team, and a process built around measurable business outcomes, not just aesthetics.

Does Now Level Up have experience working with large corporate organisations in UX/UI design?

Yes. We've partnered with established enterprises and high-growth companies across fintech, e-commerce and B2B SaaS, helping them scale complex products while keeping the experience simple for their end users.

Does Now Level Up work with startups in UX/UI design?

Absolutely. We work with early-stage founders on MVP design and rapid prototyping, and with growth-stage startups on scaling their product design as their user base expands.

Why should we onboard Now Level Up? What's special about you?

We bring design, technology and marketing together under one roof, backed by a proven track record of 170+ projects delivered. That means fewer handoffs, faster turnarounds, and a team that's invested in your product's success, not just the deliverables.

What does your UI/UX design process look like from start to finish?

We start with discovery and research, move into wireframes and information architecture, then design high-fidelity UI and interactive prototypes, and close with usability testing and handoff-ready assets for development.

How long does a typical UI/UX design project take?

Timelines vary with scope, but most projects run 4-10 weeks from kickoff to final handoff. We'll share a clear timeline once we understand your product and goals.

How many rounds of revisions are included in your design process?

We build structured feedback rounds into every phase of the project, so revisions happen early and often rather than at the very end, keeping the process collaborative and on schedule.

Do you involve clients throughout the design process?

Yes, we treat you as part of the team. You'll review work at every major milestone, from wireframes to final UI, so there are no surprises at handoff.

What tools do you use for UI/UX design and prototyping?

We work with industry-standard tools like Figma for design and prototyping, along with research and collaboration tools suited to your project's needs.

What UI/UX design services does Now Level Up offer?

Our services span UI design, UX design, UX research, UX writing, design systems, animation, iconography and illustrations — everything needed to take a product from concept to a polished, ready-to-build interface.

Do you offer UX research and usability testing?

Yes, user research and usability testing are built into our process, helping us validate design decisions with real user feedback before development begins.

Can you help redesign an existing product, or only build from scratch?

Both. We regularly audit and redesign existing products to fix usability issues and modernise the experience, alongside designing new products from the ground up.

Do you provide design systems and style guides?

Yes, we build scalable design systems and UI guidelines so your product stays visually consistent as your team and feature set grow.

Do your design services include front-end development?

Yes, alongside design we offer UI and web development, so your interface can go from prototype to a fully built, functioning product with the same team.

Do you design for both web and mobile platforms?

Yes, we design websites, web apps and native mobile apps, tailoring each experience to how users actually interact with that platform.

How is mobile app UI/UX design different from website design?

Mobile design accounts for smaller screens, touch interactions, and platform-specific navigation patterns, while web design has more room for complex layouts and needs to work across many screen sizes and browsers.

Do you design responsive websites that work across all devices?

Yes, every website we design is responsive by default, so it adapts cleanly across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Can you design for both iOS and Android apps?

Yes, we design for both platforms, adapting the interface to each one's conventions while keeping your brand experience consistent across devices.

Do you follow platform-specific design guidelines like Material Design and Apple HIG?

Yes, we design within Google's Material Design and Apple's Human Interface Guidelines where relevant, so your app feels native and intuitive on each platform.
